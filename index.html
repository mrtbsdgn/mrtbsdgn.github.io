How To Play

1
Short Tap: Reveals the target cell. Standard number clues indicate surrounding mine counts.
2
Long Press (Green Bomb): Place a Green Bomb on confirmed mine locations to collect points (+1 Point).
3
Quick Reveal (Chording): Tap a number cell surrounded by sufficient Green Bombs to instantly clear surrounding safe tiles.
4
Deadlock Resolution: If no logical moves remain, the board shifts to Purple Theme, awarding a Purple Bomb (+20 Points) to continue your journey!
